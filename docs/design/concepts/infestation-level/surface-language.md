## Keep

The seep provides a subtle early-stage marker. Tubes have distinct thicknesses and swollen junctions. The living carpet transforms the whole ground surface with a low wet membrane. Wall growth visibly joins to its ground patch while the door stays open. Tan egg ribs connect the brood study to current bug materials. Resin wraps the existing barrier rather than replacing its recognizable cover silhouette.

## Change or resolve before production

Reduce microtexture and numerous sharp specular glints into simple readable material contrasts. The eggs are a morphology study, not a scale-accurate spawner model. Decorative sacs may need closed seams or a different silhouette to avoid confusion with active objectives. The carpet needs a tactical read check beneath brown bugs and olive infantry. These studies are not seamless textures, collision specifications or production-ready 3D assets.
