Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: environment concept art for Terra Under Threat, a near-future Earth isometric tactics game. Review board for a proposed map-generation dial called Infestation Level.
Primary request: Create a polished landscape concept sheet, exactly 1536x1024, with THREE equal-width large isometric city-corner dioramas side by side, presenting three alternative approaches to a creepy insect hive overtaking human streets at the SAME infestation intensity, around level 7 of 10. The three ideas must be visually distinct in SHAPE, not just color. Each diorama is the same two-storey flat-roof concrete shop, pale square sidewalk, asphalt road, curb drain and one low concrete barrier, photographed from the same orthographic 35-degree elevated diagonal. Leave comfortable margins and readable space between scenes.
LEFT concept: "A / WET VEINS". Thick branching raised russet tendons creep outward from the gutter, adhere to walls, bifurcate across paving and gather into wet, low, slick organic mats. Viscous sick-green residue collects in a few recessed pockets. Fleshy tubes bind curb to facade; a few little blister sacs. This is visibly gooey bug biology, not tree roots or vines. Most architecture remains recognizable.
MIDDLE concept: "B / RESIN SHELL". Brown resin and layered walnut/chestnut insect carapace scutes engulf the same street corner. Broad overlapping curved shell plates, sandy tan ridges, low ribbed buttresses growing against the existing building, a few sticky threads between plates and small secretion pools. Architecture partially cocooned, sculptural and armored, grounded and not a fantasy castle.
RIGHT concept: "C / BROOD MEMBRANE". The same shop is enveloped in russet semi-opaque wet membranes and stretched fleshy connective sheets, with grouped oval brown eggs cradled in tan insect ribs along the wall, webbed curtains tucked between structural supports, and sagging mucous filaments. A few small magenta egg openings. Extremely creepy nesting place, without blood or gore. Membrane sheets hug surfaces; main street and door apertures remain visibly legible.
Style/medium: stylized, low-poly game environment concept render, broad intentional shapes, gently faceted organic surfaces, mostly flat colors, clean object edges; more sculptural detail on the infestation than the simple buildings. Not photorealism and not a cinematic illustration. Shapes must read at tactical zoom, around 64 px per two-metre tile.
Palette: base city asphalt #3A3D42, concrete #8E8A82, sidewalk #A7A297, windows #6E8FA6, roof #55524C. Infestation: russet flesh #73452E and #956344, walnut chitin #5C3B25, chestnut #8B5D36, dark umber #2E2118, tan shell ribs #B88B58 and #C6A275. Slime #4C8F1A. Tiny bright biological points only: green #9CFF3D and magenta #E23DFF; glow covers less than 3 percent. Wetness comes from a few broad specular glints on goop; hard shell stays matte.
Composition: dark neutral charcoal art-board background. The dioramas occupy the middle 80 percent of the page, each large enough to inspect. Restrained clean monospace typography. Top title exactly "INFESTATION / THREE DIRECTIONS". Subtitle exactly "LEVEL 7 / FORM STUDIES". Put each A, B, C label beneath its corresponding diorama as given above. Small bottom footer exactly "CONCEPT ART / NOT IN-GAME".
Lighting: same neutral daylight key with soft ambient in all three panels. Spooky through biological form, never darkness or fog.
Constraints: no characters, no added combat units, no tentacle monsters, no human anatomy, no gore, no alien skyscrapers, no purple ground wash, no luminous rivers, no perspective wide-angle, no UI screenshot, no watermark. Keep buildings, road geometry, camera and coverage comparable.
