Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original creature concept sheet for Terra Under Threat, a low-poly isometric tactics game.
Primary request: explore a new swarmer bug design called Splitmask. The swarmer is the small, fast, fragile melee creature encountered in large numbers. It must look dangerous and biologically alien, with a distinct, simple silhouette that could become a 600-triangle game model.
Subject: a ground-running predatory insect with a narrow arrow-shaped body, head held low, pinched waist and compact locust-like rear haunches. Exactly six limbs total: four jointed running legs and a separate front pair of short forward-pointing knife forearms, with no hands. The rear running legs are folded tight and powerful, not tall spider stilts. The defining feature is a pale split head mask: two blade-shaped chitin plates meet in a deep black vertical cleft above small recessed green eyes. Three thin overlapping ivory chevrons continue along the dark back, separated by broad dark flexible joints. A short tapered abdomen ends within the body silhouette, with no long tail. Blade arms stay short and forward, never giant scythes. Show feet bearing weight and visibly connected joints. Adult proportions, no cute eyes.
Scale: about 1 metre high and 1.6 metres long, fitting within one 2-metre square tile. Keep the torso horizontal and the entire pose low and ready to rush.
Materials and palette: dry matte chitin, broad planar bone-coloured plates #D8CBB0 over charcoal-purple #14121A and #2B2436 joints; muted #4A3B5A transition planes. Small vivid green #9CFF3D eye recesses only, with no glow spill. Ivory takes a much larger share of the upper body than on the old purple spiny bug. This is grown shell, never metal or a robot.
Style: polished game-development concept art with deliberate low-poly planes, crisp readable edges and restrained flat shading. Large designed surfaces, sparse bevels, strong negative space between limbs. No surface noise, procedural cracks, painted scratches, tiny spikes, or triangulated tessellation across every surface.
Composition: landscape 1536 by 1024 concept sheet on a uniform warm neutral grey #8E8A82. One large front three-quarter orthographic view from 35 degrees above occupies the upper two thirds, facing left. Along the bottom, two smaller studies show the exact same creature in a clean side profile facing left and a front view. Keep all bodies and blade tips inside the frame with comfortable margins; separate studies so silhouettes do not overlap. Consistent anatomy, proportions and colours across views. Soft short contact shadows, one diffuse studio key light, no environment.
Constraints: original creature design; sharp, bladed and chitinous. No wings, no humanoid torso, no mammal paws, no firearms, no heavy armoured tank proportions, no tall spine row. No text, labels, logos, borders or watermark. These are concept illustrations, not a screenshot or a 3D model validation render.
