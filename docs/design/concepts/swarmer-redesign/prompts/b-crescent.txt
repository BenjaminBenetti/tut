Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original creature concept sheet for Terra Under Threat, a low-poly isometric tactics game.
Primary request: explore a new swarmer bug design called Crescent. The swarmer is a small, fast, fragile ground-running melee creature encountered in large numbers. Give it an unfamiliar prehistoric insect silhouette that could become a 600-triangle game model.
Subject: a flattened skittering alien with a thin crescent-shaped head shield, inspired loosely by trilobites and horseshoe crabs but with a predatory insect body and no long tail. The crescent's two swept corners point backward; its front edge is a shallow curved blade, not a big pair of horns. A short narrow segmented abdomen trails behind the much broader head shield. The shell is wafer-thin at its edges with a visibly slight underside, avoiding the mass of a tank or crab monster. Exactly six limbs total: four short articulated running legs visible to either side and two separate little hooked blade forearms reaching forward from under the shield. Expose the forearm joints in the hero view so they read as limbs rather than mouth teeth. Two tiny green eye pits sit under the shield lip. Three separate pale bone lozenges lie flush down the dorsal centre with black gaps, making a simple dotted stripe rather than raised spines. Short jointed legs hug the ground, feet bearing weight, no spider stilts. Keep the outline horizontally stretched with a clear front and back.
Scale: about 1 metre high at most and 1.6 metres long within one 2-metre tile. Fast and lightly built, with a low ground clearance; no huge dome.
Materials and palette: broad matte charcoal-purple chitin #2B2436, darkest underside #14121A, raised planes #4A3B5A, a thin pale bone #D8CBB0 leading edge and three separated dorsal lozenges. Small green #9CFF3D eye pits and just two sheltered short green breathing slits near the back of the shield. No broad neon stripes, no glossy metal, no energy shield. Colour is secondary to the crescent outline.
Style: polished game-development concept art, designed low-poly planar forms with crisp edges, clean large colour blocks and restrained flat shading. Controlled faceted curves on the crescent, not a dense mesh or noisy surface. Menacing alien biology, not cute or toy-like.
Composition: landscape 1536 by 1024 on uniform warm neutral grey #8E8A82. One large front three-quarter orthographic view from 35 degrees above occupies the upper two thirds, facing left. Along the bottom show the exact same creature in a smaller clean side profile facing left and a smaller front view. Keep every leg and blade within frame with comfortable margins. No overlapping studies. Consistent limb count, anatomy, shield shape and colours across views. One soft studio key, short contact shadows, no scenery.
Constraints: original sharp bladed chitinous creature. No wings, humanoid torso, pincers, mammal paws, giant horns, long tail, weapon props or heavy tank proportions. No tall dorsal spine row. No text, labels, logos, borders or watermark. These are concept illustrations, not a screenshot or a 3D model validation render.
