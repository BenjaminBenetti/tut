# Agents

Every agent working in this repository follows `CLAUDE.md`, whichever vendor runs it. Read it first: it is the contract (hard rules, commands, conventions). `docs/process/studio.md` is the process, `docs/process/roles/<role>.md` is your role brief, and `docs/handoff/<role>.md` holds your predecessor's notes.

Skills under `.claude/skills/` are plain markdown and apply to you too; the Art Director's headless Blender workflow is `.claude/skills/art-blender/SKILL.md`.
