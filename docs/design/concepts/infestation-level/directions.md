## Keep

Three distinct types of growth are easy to compare: rounded wet tendons, overlapping carapace and stretched brood membranes. The brown biological palette fits the existing bugs. Human structures remain identifiable, while the growth visibly connects pavement to walls. Daylight makes the forms inspectable.

## Change or resolve before production

The generated materials are more detailed and glossy than the current environment. Simplify the fine cracks, wispy strands and small highlights for tactical scale. Resin plates project farther than a purely decorative cover treatment should. Brood eggs need a visual distinction from mission spawners. The three panels have broadly similar coverage, not a measured common percentage; the level-7 caption is an art target.

The fabricated shop sign and small architectural differences are incidental to the generated concept, not proposed changes to the business-sign or building kits.
