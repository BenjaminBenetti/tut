# Business signs

Commercial entrances show individual business names, with **50 names for each of nine types: 450 names total**. Seven shop identities, offices and depots retain recognizable colors and pictograms. Names include “Market My Words,” “Dough And Behold,” “Pill And Proper,” “Seams Legit,” “Ohm Sweet Ohm,” “Drill & Thrill,” “Shelf Esteem,” “Deskside Manner” and “Crate Expectations.” Existing maps with an unknown shop identity keep their generic awnings.

The [canonical catalogue](../../../src/graphics/data/business-names.json) is shared by the renderer and the offline printer. Selection uses the saved map seed and building IDs, dealing each trade's full pool before repeating. Every entrance on a building shares its name; revisiting a saved map, rotating the camera, or rearranging the building array does not rename it.

|---|---|---|
| Grocery | Green with cream lettering | Apple |
| Bakery/café | Rust with cream lettering | Steaming cup |
| Pharmacy | White with green lettering | Green cross |
| Clothing | Brick with cream lettering | Shirt |
| Electronics | Deep blue with cream lettering | Screen and lightning bolt |
| Hardware | Sand with dark lettering | Crossed tools |
| Bookshop | Dark timber with cream lettering | Open book |
| Offices | Metal grey with cream lettering | Office building |
| Depot | Muted roof green with cream lettering | Stacked packages |

The signs are part of shallow entrance canopies. Full-width modules span three tiles; compact one-tile versions retain the pictogram when a wider sign would cross a corner, overlap another canopy, or obstruct a ladder. The sign faces the real entrance's outward direction. These are wall-owned visual attachments: fog, storey cuts, unit cutaways and demolition use the existing frontage behavior. They do not alter the saved map, navigation, cover, windows or doors.

All eighteen modules are authored in Blender, with closed meshes and a wall pivot at the rear. The canopy projects 0.66 tiles and mounts 1.08 tiles above its floor; its highest point is 1.48. The lower fascia hangs at the outer edge, while the canopy's rear and brackets clear the doorway opening. Each module stays within the building-module budget of 800 triangles and 100 KiB.

The base modules retain opaque, embedded category artwork as a fallback. Named signs replace only the fascia print with pre-rendered artwork, using the same environment palette, DejaVu Sans Bold and pictograms. Each trade has five 1024×1024 texture pages, with ten padded 1024×96 labels per page. The renderer loads only pages used by the current map, shares their textures and materials, and copies the fascia UVs to select a name. Names add no models or geometry. Compact signs retain their icon because a full business name would be too small to read.

The base artwork is generated by `tools/art/models/business_sign_texture.py`; `business_sign_parts.py` supplies the shared geometry and UVs. Each `building-business-*.py` script exports one GLB; the three Blender inspection angles are saved in `docs/design/renders/`. Named artwork lives under `public/assets/textures/business-signs/names/`, with hashes binding its files and name order to the canonical catalogue.

Rebuild the name artwork after editing the catalogue, or verify it without writing files:

```sh
art-python tools/art/build-business-name-atlases.py
art-python tools/art/build-business-name-atlases.py --check
```

Rebuild a module:

```sh
blender -b --python tools/art/make_model.py -- \
  --script tools/art/models/building-business-grocery.py \
  --id building.business-grocery --category buildings \
  --file building-business-grocery.glb --quality final \
  --max-triangles 800 --no-textured
```

Use `--no-textured` to preserve the embedded print instead of applying the environment atlas. The texture helper uses the existing `art-python` environment with Pillow and the container's DejaVu font.

Production-renderer review captures use ordinary generated buildings, the roofed view, and a camera quadrant facing the entrance:

```sh
node tools/art/preview/capture-building-interiors.mjs --exteriors
```
