Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original creature concept sheet for Terra Under Threat, a low-poly isometric tactics game.
Primary request: explore a new swarmer bug design called Ribknife. It is the small, fast, fragile melee creature of the alien swarm. Explore a visibly living, sinewy insect with exposed flexible tissue and an economical silhouette that could become a 600-triangle game model.
Subject: a low forward-thrust hunting insect, with a narrow compressed flea-like thorax, tucked head, small tapered abdomen and short powerful folded rear legs. Exactly six limbs total: four articulated running legs and two separate front limbs ending in short downward-curving meat-hook blades, never hands or giant mantis scythes. Hooks sweep forward then down, visibly growing from jointed forearms under the front of the thorax. The defining motif is three broad, separated bone-coloured external ribs wrapping across a wine-red thorax; black negative spaces separate the ribs. These are intact grown exoskeleton arches protecting natural exposed muscle membranes, not wounds or a human skeleton. Keep the highest rib low and angled forward, no tall crest. A small dark angular face is tucked beneath the first rib and carries two compact green eye clusters. Small dark chitin caps cover the rear haunches. Flesh between the ribs stretches in two or three broad simple bands. All limbs visibly attach, feet bearing weight. The body reads spring-loaded and hungry; no humanoid posture or upright torso.
Scale: about 1 metre tall and 1.6 metres long, entirely within one 2-metre square tile. Crouched running stance, horizontal overall silhouette, slim waist, lightly built and vulnerable rather than heavily armoured. No tail beyond the short abdomen.
Materials and palette: dry bone #D8CBB0 external ribs and hook cutting edges; matte dark chitin #14121A and #2B2436 limb caps and blade backs; muted wine-red #7A3A4E and #B05A6E exposed biological membranes. Small green #9CFF3D eyes only, no luminous ribs or glow spill. No wet gore, blood or wounds. Rib shape and exposed membrane make this visually distinct from a fully armoured purple bug.
Style: polished game-development concept art with deliberate low-poly planes, large crisp colour blocks, restrained flat shading and very selective organic curves. Slightly unsettling anatomy that remains clear at small size. No photoreal pores, surface scratches, tiny tendons, repeated micro-spikes or dense triangulated tessellation. Not cute, not a mammal or reptile.
Composition: landscape 1536 by 1024 on uniform warm neutral grey #8E8A82. One large front three-quarter orthographic view from 35 degrees above occupies the upper two thirds, facing left. Along the bottom show two smaller studies of the exact same design: a clean side profile facing left and a front view. Preserve the same three ribs and six limbs in every view. Comfortable margins, blade tips completely inside frame, no overlapping studies. One diffuse studio key, short soft contact shadows, no scenery.
Constraints: original sharp bladed chitinous creature; no humanoid chest or hands, no wings, no giant scythes, no long tail, no spider stilts, no heavy tank proportions. No text, labels, logos, borders or watermark. These are concept illustrations, not a screenshot or a 3D model validation render.
